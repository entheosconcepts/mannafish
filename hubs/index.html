Ask for a Hub

Places that may want to host MannaFish. Tick every place you would like, then ask. One request per person per place; sign in to ask. When a place has enough requests, it qualifies, and we talk with them about becoming a Hub. A Hub may draw new people through its door, the way a return counter does.
