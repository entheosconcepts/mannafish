How to play

  1. Pick a first and a second language at the top (or Mix).
  2. Tap two fish to turn them over.
  3. The same word is a match, even in two different languages: REST and DESCANSA are a pair. A match stays face up.
  4. Not a match? They turn back over. Remember where each word was.
  5. Every pair lets you go fish.
  6. Find all 12 pairs. Tap a turned fish again to see its word in the other language; tap the middle of the fish (🔊) to hear the word, and the lips at its mouth to hear its verse.
  7. More players?

Tap any card to hear its verse.
