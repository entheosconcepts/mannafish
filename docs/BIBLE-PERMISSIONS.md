## The order to ask in

| # | Who | What you get | How hard | How |

| 4 | **Faith Comes By Hearing: Bible Brain** | **The biggest for other languages:** Bible text in about 1,400+ languages, *and* recorded audio Bibles (this also covers the audio upgrade on the list) | Free for ministries, as long as users never pay | Request a key on the Bible Brain page (faithcomesbyhearing.com, under Audio Bible Resources, then Bible Brain). Use **Letter B** in the "tell us about your project" box. |
| 5 | **HarperCollins Christian Publishing** (NKJV; NIV in the US with Biblica) | NKJV, NIV | Harder: 6 to 8 weeks, online form only, no phone | **harpercollinschristian.com/permissions**, the permission request form. Paste **Letter C**. |
| 6 | **Biblica** (NIV worldwide) | NIV, plus Biblica's translations in many languages | Harder | **biblica.com/permissions**. Paste **Letter C**. |
| 7 | **The Lockman Foundation** | NASB, Amplified | Harder: form | **lockman.org**, then "Permission to Quote", then the Request Form. Paste **Letter C**. |
| 8 | **Holman Bible Publishers** (Lifeway) | CSB | Harder: written request | Holman Bible Publishers, One LifeWay Plaza, Nashville, TN 37234 (or their permissions page). **Letter C**. |

**Sponsors (Ken asked, 27 Oct).** Keep sponsor names and logos away from any licensed Bible text (a sponsor page on its
own is fine). The printed fish use the public-domain KJV, so they are unaffected. The letters mention sponsors up front.

**One honest point.** Most free licenses are for *non-commercial* use. MannaFish is free, with no ads, no fees and nothing to
buy, so say that plainly. Sponsors and donations are gifts to the ministry, not sales of Scripture. If a publisher asks, the
printed fish and decals are also given away free.

We are grateful for what the American Bible Society does to get Scripture to every person in their own language.
