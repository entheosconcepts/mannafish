# Time with God: the text the audio reads now

This is the existing text, step by step, in the order the listener hears it. Edit anything you like and send the file (or the changed parts) back; nothing in the app or the audio changes until you approve. Each entry shows the Bible verse that is read first, then the words the voice speaks.

Order of the steps: Be still, Honor God, Today's word, Give thanks, My concerns, Pray for someone, My response, Listen, Closing.

## 3. Today's word (the same line every day)
> Pray today's word over your day: Lord, teach me to [the word]. Let this word go with me today, and give me someone to share it with.

## 1. Be still
(13 entries; the app picks one at random each time)

### 1
*Verse read first (Psalm 46:10, BSB):* “Be still and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted over the earth.”
**Spoken:** Be still. Let your shoulders drop and take a slow breath. God is here with you right now, and He is not in a hurry.

### 2
*Verse read first (Exodus 33:14, BSB):* And the LORD answered, “My Presence will go with you, and I will give you rest.”
**Spoken:** Before you say anything, simply rest. The Lord has promised that His presence will go with you. Breathe in, breathe out, and let your heart settle.

### 3
*Verse read first (Psalm 62:1, BSB):* In God alone my soul finds rest; my salvation comes from Him.
**Spoken:** Find a quiet place and let the noise fade. Your soul can rest in God alone. He has been waiting to meet with you.

### 4
*Verse read first (Matthew 11:28, BSB):* Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.
**Spoken:** Come as you are, tired or full of questions. Jesus invites you to come to Him and rest. Take a deep breath and let Him hold the weight for a moment.

### 5
*Verse read first (Isaiah 30:15, BSB):* For the Lord GOD, the Holy One of Israel, has said: “By repentance and rest you would be saved; your strength would lie in quiet confidence— but you were not willing.”
**Spoken:** In quietness and trust you will find strength. Slow down. You do not have to earn this time; just be here with Him.

### 6
*Verse read first (Psalm 139:7, BSB):* Where can I go to escape Your Spirit? Where can I flee from Your presence?
**Spoken:** There is nowhere you can go where God is not already there. Breathe slowly. He is closer than your next breath.

### 7
*Verse read first (Zephaniah 3:17, BSB):* The LORD your God is among you; He is mighty to save. He will rejoice over you with gladness; He will quiet you with His love; He will rejoice over you with singing.”
**Spoken:** The Lord your God is with you, and He delights in you. Let that settle over you as you breathe deeply. You are welcome here.

### 8
*Verse read first (Psalm 16:11, BSB):* You have made known to me the path of life; You will fill me with joy in Your presence, with eternal pleasures at Your right hand.
**Spoken:** Pause and notice the quiet around you. In God’s presence there is fullness of joy. Let the rush of the day wait outside for a while.

### 9
*Verse read first (Deuteronomy 31:8, BSB):* The LORD Himself goes before you; He will be with you. He will never leave you nor forsake you. Do not be afraid or discouraged.”
**Spoken:** Take a breath and remember that the Lord goes before you and will never leave you. Quiet your thoughts. He is already here.

### 10
*Verse read first (Lamentations 3:25–26, BSB):* The LORD is good to those who wait for Him, to the soul who seeks Him. It is good to wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD.
**Spoken:** It is good to wait quietly for the Lord. Close your eyes if you like and breathe slowly. Let your heart turn toward Him.

### 11
*Verse read first (James 4:8, BSB):* Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you double-minded.
**Spoken:** Draw near to God, and He will draw near to you. Set down your phone and your to-do list. Breathe, and know that He is listening.

### 12
*Verse read first (Psalm 23:2–3, BSB):* He makes me lie down in green pastures; He leads me beside quiet waters. He restores my soul; He guides me in the paths of righteousness for the sake of His name.
**Spoken:** Picture green pastures and still waters. Your Shepherd leads you there to restore your soul. Take a slow breath and rest in His care.

### 13
*Verse read first (Matthew 28:20, BSB):* …and teaching them to obey all that I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”
**Spoken:** Jesus promised to be with you always. Let that promise slow you down. Breathe in His peace and breathe out your hurry.

## 5. My concerns
(31 entries; the app picks one at random each time)

### 1
*Verse read first (Philippians 4:6, BSB):* Be anxious for nothing, but in everything, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God.
**Spoken guide:** God invites you to bring Him everything, not just the big things. Picture yourself opening your hands and placing each worry in His. His peace will guard your heart as you let go.
**Then it asks:** What is weighing on you today?

### 2
*Verse read first (1 Peter 5:7, BSB):* Cast all your anxiety on Him, because He cares for you.
**Spoken guide:** Imagine picking up each worry like a stone and placing it on Jesus’ strong shoulders. He is not bothered by your cares; He cares for you. Let each one go as you name it.
**Then it asks:** Which worries can you cast on Him right now?

### 3
*Verse read first (Matthew 11:28–30, BSB):* Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me; for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For My yoke is easy and My burden is light.”
**Spoken guide:** Picture a heavy backpack you have been carrying for days. Jesus kneels beside you, unbuckles the straps, and lifts it off your shoulders. His yoke is easy and His burden is light.
**Then it asks:** What is in your backpack today?

### 4
*Verse read first (Mark 4:39, BSB):* Then Jesus got up and rebuked the wind and the sea. “Silence!” He commanded. “Be still!” And the wind died down, and it was perfectly calm.
**Spoken guide:** Think of the disciples in the boat as the waves crashed in. Jesus stood up, spoke to the wind, and the sea grew calm. He is in your boat too.
**Then it asks:** What storm do you need Him to calm today?

### 5
*Verse read first (Isaiah 40:11, BSB):* He tends His flock like a shepherd; He gathers the lambs in His arms and carries them close to His heart. He gently leads the nursing ewes.
**Spoken guide:** Picture a shepherd gathering a tired lamb into his arms and holding it close to his heart. That is how Jesus carries you. You do not have to walk this road alone.
**Then it asks:** Where do you need to be carried today?

### 6
*Verse read first (Psalm 55:22, BSB):* Cast your burden upon the LORD and He will sustain you; He will never let the righteous be shaken.
**Spoken guide:** Imagine setting your burden down at the Lord’s feet, the way you set down heavy bags at the door. He promises to sustain you. He will not let you fall.
**Then it asks:** What burden can you set down now?

### 7
*Verse read first (John 14:27, BSB):* Peace I leave with you; My peace I give to you. I do not give to you as the world gives. Do not let your hearts be troubled; do not be afraid.
**Spoken guide:** Jesus tells you not to let your heart be troubled. Picture Him sitting across from you, looking at you with kindness, ready to hear it all. Tell Him what troubles you as you would tell a trusted friend.
**Then it asks:** What is troubling your heart?

### 8
*Verse read first (Matthew 6:34, BSB):* Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about itself. Today has enough trouble of its own.
**Spoken guide:** Many of our worries live in tomorrow. Picture placing each tomorrow worry in a box and handing it to the One who is already there. Today, you only need grace for today.
**Then it asks:** What worries about tomorrow can you give Him?

### 9
*Verse read first (Psalm 56:3, BSB):* When I am afraid, I put my trust in You.
**Spoken guide:** Fear is real, but you do not have to face it alone. Imagine a child reaching for a parent’s hand in the dark. Reach for God’s hand now; He is already holding yours.
