## Words to look at twice (my notes)

- **Old English, may need explaining:** Succour (help), Salute (greet), Tarry (wait), Quicken (make alive), Quit ("quit you like men": act bravely), Reprove, Esteem.
- **The verse uses another form of the word:** Hide (Psalm 119:11, "Thy word have I hid in mine heart"), Prefer ("preferring"), Season ("seasoned"), Enlighten ("enlightened"), Glean, Dress.
- **Could be read the wrong way:** Hate (Psalm 97:10, "hate evil"), Covet (1 Cor 12:31, "covet earnestly the best gifts"), Chase, Chasten, Handle, Tremble, Wipe, Glory (also a noun), Number, Root, Mend.
