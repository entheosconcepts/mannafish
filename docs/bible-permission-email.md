**Subject:** Permission request: [version] verses on free Scripture decals (MannaFish)

Hello,

I am writing for Entheos Concepts, which makes MannaFish, a free Scripture and word-study project (manna-fish.com). I would like to ask permission to use the [VERSION] for the following.

**What we make.** A MannaFish is a vinyl decal on a 4.5 x 2.75 inch card. In the middle of a fish shape is one word of Scripture, with its verse around it (one to three verses). The back of the card is a word study of that word. The decals are given away free and are never sold. Churches, businesses and individuals sponsor the printing so that people can receive them at no cost.

**What we would like to print and show.**
1. A single passage of one to three verses on each decal, with the reference and "([VERSION])" or the notice you require.
2. Print runs of about 100 to 1,000 decals per design, with new designs added over time.
3. The same verse shown on our website and Bible app (manna-fish.com) so a sponsor can see their card before it is printed.

**Questions.**
1. Is this use allowed without a licence, or what licence do you offer?
2. Are there fees, or a limit on the number of verses or decals?
3. Does a free decal paid for by a sponsor count as a commercial product for your purposes, even though it is never sold?
4. What exact copyright notice should appear on the card and the website?
5. Could you confirm your answer in writing so we can keep it on file?
