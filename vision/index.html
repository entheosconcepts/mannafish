The vision

Scripture was never meant to be kept. MannaFish puts a single word of it where you will see it, on a decal, a card, a phone, and lets you give it on. A word can start a conversation, a prayer, a gathering.

Our hope is a gift that moves: each fish is passed from one hand to the next, and each one carries the verse it came from.

“Freely ye have received, freely give.”
Matthew 10:8

How it works

1. Receive

A sponsor, a church, a shop or a friend gave you this fish. It cost you nothing.

2. Read

Open the Bible to the verse around your word, in many translations, and listen to it read aloud.

3. Give

Keep one, give one away. Pass on a fish with its verse, and say what the word meant to you.

Always free

The fish are never sold and never resold. Sponsors pay so that they can be given away; the person who receives one pays nothing.

Gatherings

This is a place for the gatherings that grow around the fish: coffee tables, church lobbies, small groups. We will share where they meet and how to start one here.
