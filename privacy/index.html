Privacy & Terms

MannaFish is a free word-study devotional and decal ministry of Yadah Collaborative. We keep what you share with us to the least we need, we use it only to serve you, and we never sell it.

What we collect, and why

  • Devotional sign-ups: your name (if you give it), email address or mobile number, how often you want the devotional, your language and time zone, so it arrives at 7am where you are.
  • Free decal requests: your name, email and mailing address, so we can mail the decals and show your request under "My decal orders".
  • Your MannaFish account (optional): your email address for signing in, and the notes, recently viewed words and "where you left off" that you choose to save.
  • Every Tongue and "hear it": words you type or speak to be translated or read aloud are sent to our translation and voice service to do that, and are not used for anything else. Recordings of what was said may be kept so the same words play faster next time.
  • On your device: your settings (language, voice, layout) and notes made without an account stay in your own browser.

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other websites, and we do not sell, rent or trade your information.

Text messages (SMS)

Program: MannaFish Daily Devotional.

What you get: a short devotional text with a Bible verse and a link to read more. Daily (Sunday through Friday), weekly (Sundays) or monthly (the first Sunday), as you choose when you sign up.

Cost: the devotional is free. Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier.

Stop any time: reply STOP to any message. You will receive one message confirming you are unsubscribed, and no more after that.

Help: reply HELP, or email 2fish@manna-fish.com.

Your number stays with us: no mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

You sign up by choosing "Text message" on the devotional form at manna-fish.com and entering your mobile number. You will only receive texts you asked for.

Email

Devotional emails come from MannaFish <2fish@manna-fish.com>. Every email has an "Unsubscribe" link that stops them with one click. Sign-in emails are sent only when you ask for one.

Who helps us run MannaFish

We use a few trusted services, only to run the site and send what you asked for:

  • Netlify hosts the website and receives the forms.
  • Supabase keeps MannaFish accounts, notes and decal requests.
  • Brevo sends the devotional emails and texts.
  • OpenAI provides the natural voices and the Every Tongue translation.
  • bible-api.com provides the English Bible chapters in the reader; Google Fonts provides the lettering.

Each of them handles your information only for that job. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Stop texts by replying STOP; stop emails with the Unsubscribe link.
  • Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it and your account: email 2fish@manna-fish.com. We reply within 30 days.
  • Clear what is kept on your device by clearing this site's data in your browser.

MannaFish is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. Information is kept only as long as needed to serve you, and is protected with secure connections and access limited to those who run the ministry.
