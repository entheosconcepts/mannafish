Choose a word. Add your link and your logo. See your card as you build it. MannaFish decals are always given away, never sold: sponsors pay so people can have them free.

Your sponsorship

Each design is its own card. Pick a card to work on it, or add another. Everything is saved as you go.

1 Choose your word

This is the big word in the middle of the fish. Its word study (the Greek and Hebrew, and four verses) fills in by itself on the back.

The fish comes with its own verse. Choose another from the list, or choose "Another verse" to type its address. The big word has to be in the verse.

These versions are all in the public domain. NIV, ESV, NLT and other versions need written permission from their publishers, and we are asking for it.

2 Your QR code

The left QR code opens your link. The right one always opens the free MannaFish devotional.

This code opens the free MannaFish Bible app, so the person who scans it reads Scripture and the devotional there.

3 Sponsored by

This goes in the middle of the card, under the top line you choose.

One line. A longer line is squeezed in width to fit above your logo, so the logo stays full size. Try: A free gift made possible by K-Tech Construction.

This fills in "This gift is made possible by our monthly donors for the MannaFish Bible app", the logo, and sends the QR code to the app.

4 How many decals?

Choose 100, 500 or 1,000 for this card. Each different design is its own card, so another word means another card (add one at the top).

You get the decals and give them out yourself. To give them through a place instead, choose it from the list: we invite that place to be a MannaFish Hub, and if they accept, we send the decals, with a basket, to them. Giving to two or more places? Use "Copy this card" above, and choose a different place on each (100 or more for each).

5 Who are you?

We email you a proof to approve before anything is printed. Nothing is charged here.

This is your card at its cut size, 4.5 × 2.75 inches. The front is the fish decal over its printed card; the back has the word study, your QR code and your sponsor line. The words and verses are filled in for you.
