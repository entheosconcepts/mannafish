Sponsor MannaFish decals

Choose a word. Add a link and a logo. See the card as it is built. MannaFish decals are always given away, never sold: sponsors pay so people can have them free.

Sponsorship

Choose the quantity, then how many different words, and who chooses them. Each word has its own tab with its own quantity. Everything is saved as it goes.

At least 100 of each word, so 500 decals can be up to 5 different words. Each word gets its own tab below. The link, the logo and the sponsor line can be the same on every word, or changed on any word.

1 Choose a word

This is the big word in the middle of the fish. Its word study (the Greek and Hebrew, and four verses) fills in by itself on the back.

The fish comes with its own verse. Choose another from the list, or choose "Another verse" to type its address. The big word has to be in the verse.

Choose which words of the verse go on the fish

Choose a verse first. Then slide to pick where the verse starts, where it breaks between the top and the bottom of the fish, and where it ends. You can also type the lines.

These versions are all in the public domain. NIV, ESV, NLT and other versions need written permission from their publishers, and we are asking for it.

2 QR code

The left QR code opens the sponsor's link. The right one always opens the free MannaFish devotional.

This code opens the free MannaFish Bible app, so the person who scans it reads Scripture and the devotional there.

3 Sponsored by

This goes in the middle of the card, under the top line you choose.

One line. A longer line is squeezed in width to fit above the logo, so the logo stays full size. Try: A free gift made possible by K-Tech Construction.

Stretch, size and place the sponsor line

Move the sliders and watch the card. Nothing is limited: the line can go over the QR codes if it is stretched far enough.

This fills in "This gift is made possible by our monthly donors for the MannaFish Bible app", the logo, and sends the QR code to the app.

4 How many of this word?

Choose 100, 500 or 1,000 of this word. At least 100 of each.

The decals come to the sponsor to give out. To give them through a place instead, choose it from the list: we invite that place to be a MannaFish Hub, and if they accept, we send the decals, with a basket, to them. Giving to two or more places? Use "Copy this card" above, and choose a different place on each (100 or more for each).

5 Contact

A proof is emailed for approval before anything is printed. Nothing is charged here.
