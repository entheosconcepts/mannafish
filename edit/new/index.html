New fish edit sheet

128 new fish for the Bible in a year, drawn live in your design. Tap the word on a fish to move it or pinch it to size, or type the numbers. Size each verse line. When one looks right, tap 📚 to make it the rule for every word with that many letters (or every line that long), or ⭐ Use as template to do all three at once. Then tap Send all changes.

The rules right now

  • The reference on the tail is always size 168, and the MannaFish™ logo is always the same size, on every fish. A long reference is shortened (Gen. instead of Genesis), never made smaller.
  • Without a rule, each verse line runs the length of its curve like your original 24 (size 170 to 230), and the word fills the eye.
  • A rule for one letter count also guides the counts in between: a 5-letter word sits between your 4- and 6-letter rules. A word never grows wider than the eye.
