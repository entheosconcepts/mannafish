The rules · copy · undo
  • The reference on the tail and the MannaFish™ logo are always the same size on every fish (168). A long reference is shortened, never made smaller.
  • New fish: each verse line runs the length of its curve like your original 24 (size 170 to 230), and the word fills the eye, unless a rule or your own setting says otherwise.
  • 📚 saves a word's size and place as the rule for every word with that many letters in that language. Fish you haven't adjusted follow it.
