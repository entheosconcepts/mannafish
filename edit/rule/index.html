Width: how much of the fish’s eye the word fills

A 5-letter word fills about 62%. Short words are set as wide as this, so their letters come out taller; a lower number keeps very short words smaller. “Go” (2 letters) was too big at 62%.

Height: the tallest a word’s letters may be

Compared with a 7-letter word (1.00). 3 letters at 1.25 means a quarter taller at most.
