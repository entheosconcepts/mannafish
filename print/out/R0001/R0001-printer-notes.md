# Yadah invitation · Live (first batch, mock-up) (run R0001)

Card: 4.5 x 2.75 in (114.3 x 69.85 mm), custom size (not 2.8 in). Every file includes a 1/8 in (3.175 mm) bleed on each side, so the fish-side files are 120.65 x 76.2 mm and the back is 76.2 x 120.65 mm. All files line up with each other at the same size.

1. **R0001-1-decal-white-ink** : clear vinyl, white ink only. White where the fish is white; the letters and everything else are clear (no ink).
2. **R0001-2-backing-front** : printed on the FRONT of the backing paper, under the decal (not the back of the paper). Black over the whole card, the same fish in white, lined up exactly under the decal.
3. **R0001-3-cut-lines** : blue (layer "KissCut") is the kiss cut, through the vinyl only, 1/8 in outside the fish. Pink (layer "CardCut") is the card edge, cut through everything.
4. **R0001-4-back** : printed on the BACK of the backing paper. It reads upright when the card is turned on its side (portrait).

Quantity: 100. Please send a proof showing all four before taking payment.
