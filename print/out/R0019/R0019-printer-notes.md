# FORMAT EXAMPLE · Trust (longer definitions wrap to a second line) (run R0019)

Card: 4.5 x 2.75 in (114.3 x 69.85 mm), custom size (not 2.8 in). Every file includes a 1/8 in (3.175 mm) bleed on each side, so the fish-side files are 120.65 x 76.2 mm and the back is 120.65 x 76.2 mm. All files line up with each other at the same size.

1. **R0019-1-decal-white-ink** : clear vinyl, white ink only. White where the fish is white; the letters and everything else are clear (no ink).
2. **R0019-2-backing-front** : printed on the FRONT of the backing paper, under the decal (not the back of the paper). Black over the whole card, the same fish in white, lined up exactly under the decal.
3. **Cut lines (R0015-3-cut-lines)** : THE FRONT DECAL IS KISS CUT. Cut through the clear vinyl only, in the shape of the fish, about 1/8 in (3 mm) or less outside the fish (magenta CutContour). Do not cut the backing paper there, so the card stays whole when the decal is peeled off. Then cut the card edge all the way through at 4.5 x 2.75 in (blue ThroughCut). You know the right distance for your machines; our cut line file is ready if you would rather use it.
4. **R0019-4-back** : printed on the BACK of the backing paper. It reads upright held the same way as the fish side (landscape).

The decal's white ink is the PNG (1200 dpi, transparent where there is no ink); the PDF holds the same picture. Colours are RGB: please convert to your press colours, and use a rich black for the black areas if that is what you recommend.

Quantity: 100. Please send a proof showing all four before taking payment.
